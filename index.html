About me

Analytical thinking, commercial context.

My work sits at the intersection of data, fraud and product. I investigate customer behaviour and emerging abuse patterns, assess the performance of risk controls, and turn findings into practical recommendations for product, data science and operational teams.

I’m particularly interested in the balance between preventing financial loss and reducing unnecessary friction for genuine customers. Good analysis should do more than explain what happened: it should help teams decide what to change, how to measure the impact and where to focus next.

This portfolio is a home for independent projects using public datasets, with a focus on clear analysis, useful visualisations and the decisions the evidence can support.
